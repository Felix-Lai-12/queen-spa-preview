How do I arrange a visit?＋

Use “Book your ritual” to choose a treatment, duration and number of guests. Add your preferred date and time, then review your contact details. The spa must confirm the appointment and any additional charges.

What is included in the price?＋

The menu shows the treatment price per person in Vietnamese dong. Your request summary calculates the treatment subtotal for your party. Taxes, tips and other inclusions need confirmation before your visit.

Can I choose the massage pressure?＋

Discuss your preferences and any concerns directly with the spa before choosing a treatment. Tell your therapist if the pressure or temperature feels uncomfortable.

What are the opening hours?＋

Opening hours to be confirmed. Choose a preferred time in the booking form; it is a request, not a live availability slot. Confirm the appointment and cancellation terms before travelling.
